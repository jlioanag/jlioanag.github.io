# Backpacking Desolation Wilderness, CA - Postmortem

## Went well

* Weather was great (high 60F - low 40F)
* Optimal pack weight, not too heavy given first time with bear canister
* Good layering (base, mid-layer, insulation)
* Good pace on trail
* Cnoc 2L water bag was great for gravity filtering lakewater
* Wool socks saved my feet and don't stink

## Went not so well

* Each individual should carry their waste *(in bear can)*
* Forgot lighter, had to borrow one
* Boots can be bothersome: laces caught on buckles
* Need water and more snacks for doing dayhikes

## Next time

* Potentially pack a small dayhike bag
* Get stove with piezo-ignition
* Not every grocery will have Smartwater - consider a reusable alternative
